Important — No Separate AIIMS Exam

From 2020 onwards: There is NO separate AIIMS entrance exam. All AIIMS MBBS admissions happen exclusively through NEET UG scores. Appear for NEET UG → Clear cutoff → Apply through MCC counselling for AIIMS.

AIIMS Delhi — Key Facts

  • Ranked #1 in India (NIRF Medical Ranking — 8 consecutive years)
  • 132 MBBS seats total (including reserved categories)
  • Hospital with 2,500+ beds — massive clinical exposure
  • Research opportunities from first year — publications, projects
  • No state quota — 100% All India competition. Pure merit.
  • Hostel available on campus at very subsidized rates
  • MD/MS/DM/MCh specialization options after MBBS at AIIMS itself
