IIMC — Indian Institute of Mass Communication

IIMC (Indian Institute of Mass Communication), New Delhi, is India's premier journalism and mass communication institute. Established by the Government of India in 1965. It offers postgraduate diploma programs in journalism, advertising and public relations, radio and TV journalism, and new media.

Important: IIMC programs are postgraduate (after graduation). For Class 12 students, the goal should be to get into a good undergraduate journalism/mass comm program first, then aim for IIMC after graduation.

Civil Services (UPSC) — Open to Arts Graduates

The UPSC Civil Services Exam (IAS/IPS/IFS/IRS) is one of the most prestigious career paths available to Arts graduates. Humanities subjects like History, Political Science, Geography, Psychology, Sociology, and Public Administration are popular optional subjects for UPSC.

DetailInformation
EligibilityAny graduation degree from a recognized university. No minimum percentage. Age: 21–32 years (Gen).
Exam StructurePrelims (objective) → Mains (descriptive, 9 papers) → Interview/Personality Test
Number of AttemptsGeneral: 6 attempts. OBC: 9 attempts. SC/ST: unlimited till age limit.
Best UG for UPSCBA History / Political Science / Geography / Psychology / Sociology / Public Administration from a good university
Typical timelineGraduation (3yr) + UPSC prep (1-3yr) + Prelims → Mains → Interview. Total 4-6 years after Class 12.
Salary (IAS Officer)Starting: ~₹56,100/month + HA + DA. District Collector level: ₹1.5–2 Lakh/month equivalent. Non-monetary perks: residence, vehicle, staff, pension.
Arts advantage in UPSC: Many top UPSC rankers have Arts/Humanities backgrounds. History, Political Science, and Geography are among the most popular and scoring optional subjects in UPSC Mains.

BA Psychology → Clinical Psychologist / Counsellor

  • Route: BA Psychology (3yr) → MA Psychology (2yr) → RCI registration (for clinical practice) or MPhil Clinical Psychology
  • Eligibility: Class 12 any stream. Psychology available at DU, JNU, Hyderabad University via CUET
  • Career: School counsellor, hospital clinical psychologist, HR (organizational psychology), corporate wellness, private practice
  • Salary: Starting ₹25,000–40,000/month. Private practice: ₹1,500–5,000 per session (experienced practitioners)
  • Growing field: Mental health awareness has made psychology one of the fastest-growing career fields in India post-2020
