NID — National Institute of Design

NID is India's premier design institution — declared an Institute of National Importance. It offers a 4-year B.Des (Bachelor of Design) program across multiple specializations. Admission is through the NID Design Aptitude Test (DAT) — a two-stage exam testing creative and design aptitude.

NID Eligibility

CriteriaDetails
StreamAny stream — Science, Commerce, Arts all eligible
QualificationClass 12 passed from any recognized board. No minimum percentage required.
Age LimitUnder 20 years on July 1 of admission year (Gen/EWS). SC/ST/OBC: 3-year relaxation. PwD: 5-year relaxation.
AttemptsMultiple attempts allowed as long as age and educational criteria are met

NID DAT Exam Pattern

StageTypeTestsWhen
PrelimsWritten exam (offline)Design aptitude, visual observation, analytical ability, sketching and drawing questionsDecember 2025 (for 2026-27)
Mains (Studio Test)Hands-on studio test + Personal InterviewPractical creative tasks, model making, rapid sketching, creative problem solvingApril–May 2026
No textbook preparation: NID DAT cannot be cracked through rote learning. It tests inherent design aptitude — observation skills, visualization, creative thinking. Regular sketching practice and visual awareness are key.

NIFT — National Institute of Fashion Technology

NIFT is India's premier fashion and design institute with 18 campuses across the country. It offers programs in Fashion Design, Textile Design, Fashion Communication, Fashion Management, and Knitwear Design. Admission is through NIFT Entrance Exam conducted by NTA.

DetailInformation
EligibilityClass 12 passed, any stream. No minimum percentage for most programs.
Age LimitMaximum 23 years for UG programs (Gen). SC/ST/OBC: 3-year relaxation.
Exam PatternGAT (General Ability Test) — 100 questions, 1 hour. CAT (Creative Ability Test) — Practical drawing/design test, 3 hours. Both tests mandatory.
Conducted byNTA (National Testing Agency) on behalf of NIFT
Exam Date (2026)February 9, 2026

NIFT Placements

NIFT Delhi and Mumbai are the most sought-after campuses. Top recruiters include Reliance Trends, Aditya Birla Fashion, Arvind, Madura Fashion, Myntra, Zara India, H&M, Fabindia, and various export houses. Starting salary: ₹4–8 LPA for most graduates. Fashion Communication and Fashion Management graduates moving into digital marketing/e-commerce can earn ₹8–15 LPA. International placements available for top rankers.

NIFT vs NID: NIFT is specialized in fashion and textiles. NID is broader — product, graphic, animation, exhibition, and furniture design. If your interest is fashion specifically, NIFT is the right choice. For broader industrial or communication design, NID is better.
