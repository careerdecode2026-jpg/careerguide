Why IIIT Hyderabad is Different

IIIT Hyderabad (International Institute of Information Technology, Hyderabad) is an autonomous deemed university — not part of the NIT/IIT system. It is widely considered one of the top 3 colleges in India for Computer Science alongside IIT Bombay and IIT Delhi, particularly for students interested in research and AI/ML.

Key fact: IIIT Hyderabad does NOT admit through JoSAA. It has its own separate exam — UGEE — with a completely different selection process. Many students miss this opportunity because they don't know about it.

Admission Process — Two Routes

IIIT Hyderabad has TWO routes for B.Tech admission — both through UGEE but different components.
RouteNameComponentsWho Should Apply
Route 1UGEE — JEE BasedJEE Main score + UGEE Aptitude Test (online)Students with strong JEE Main scores
Route 2UGEE — SPEC (Research Mode)Research Aptitude Test + InterviewStudents interested in research track (dual degree B.Tech + MS)

UGEE Exam Pattern

ComponentDetails
JEE Main ScoreUsed as initial filter — minimum ~95 percentile recommended for serious consideration
UGEE Aptitude TestOnline test: Mathematics + Analytical Reasoning + Computer Science basics. Duration: ~2 hours.
SPEC (Research Route)Research aptitude paper + Personal interview. Tests curiosity, problem-solving depth.
