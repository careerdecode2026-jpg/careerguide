What is IPMAT?

IPMAT (Integrated Programme in Management Aptitude Test) is the entrance exam for the 5-year IPM program at IIM Indore and IIM Rohtak. After completing the program, students receive a degree equivalent to an MBA — without needing to give CAT or any other postgraduate entrance exam. It was the first such program in India, introduced by IIM Indore in 2011.

Who should consider IPMAT? Students who are confident about a management career and don't want to spend 3-4 years after graduation preparing for CAT. The 5-year program is intensive but gives a direct route to an IIM degree.

Other IIMs Accepting IPMAT Indore Score

  • IIM Ranchi — IPM program, ~120 seats, accepts IPMAT Indore score
  • IIM Amritsar — IPM program, ~60 seats, accepts IPMAT Indore score
  • IIM Sirmaur — BMS program, accepts IPMAT Indore score
  • IIM Bodh Gaya & IIM Jammu — JIPMAT (separate exam, easier than IPMAT Indore)
