What is JoSAA?

JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) is the single centralised counselling system for all government-funded premier engineering institutes in India. After JEE results, ALL admissions to IITs, NITs, IIITs and GFTIs happen exclusively through JoSAA — there is no direct or separate admission process.

Step-by-Step Registration Process

1

Register on josaa.nic.in

Opens June 2-3, 2026. Login with your JEE Main / JEE Advanced roll number and password. Complete registration with personal and academic details. Upload photo and signature.

2

Fill College + Branch Preferences

This is the most important step. You can fill up to 300+ choices. List every college-branch combination you would be happy with — ordered from most preferred to least preferred.

  • Fill ALL realistic options — don't leave any out
  • Include both IIT and NIT options if you have JEE Advanced rank
  • Include home state NIT in preferences — lower cutoff via Home State quota
  • You cannot change preferences after the deadline — fill carefully
3

Round 1 Seat Allotment

JoSAA allots you the best seat from your preference list that your rank qualifies for. You then choose one of four options — Freeze, Float, Slide, or Withdraw.

4

Pay Seat Acceptance Fee

Pay online to confirm your participation. Fee varies by category — approximately ₹35,000 (Gen/EWS/OBC) or ₹15,000 (SC/ST/PwD). This fee adjusts against final institute fee.

5

Rounds 2-6 — Seat May Improve

Each round, if you selected Float or Slide, JoSAA tries to give you a better allotment. If a better seat is available, your allotment upgrades automatically. If not, you keep current seat.

6

Final Reporting to Institute

After Round 6, report physically to your allotted institute with original documents. Pay remaining fees. Admission confirmed. Miss this deadline = seat cancelled permanently.

Float vs Freeze vs Slide — Explained Simply

After each round allotment, you must choose one of these options. This is where most students get confused:

🤝 FLOAT (Recommended)

Keep your current seat AND let JoSAA try to give you something better from your higher preferences in the next round. If upgrade available → you get it. If not → current seat stays. Best option in early rounds.

❓ FREEZE

Lock your current seat permanently. No more upgrades. Choose this only when you are 100% happy with your allotment and don't want to risk anything. Use in final round or when fully satisfied.

⇄ SLIDE

Try to upgrade to a better branch within the SAME institute only. Useful if you got a lower branch at your preferred institute and want to try for CSE/ECE there. Institute stays same, only branch upgrades.

❌ WITHDRAW

Exit JoSAA completely. Seat acceptance fee is forfeited. Only do this if you have confirmed admission elsewhere (BITS, private college etc.) and are 100% sure.

Simple strategy: Select FLOAT in rounds 1-4. Switch to FREEZE in round 5 or 6 once you are satisfied. Never Withdraw unless you have a confirmed better option elsewhere.

Home State Quota — Important Advantage

For NITs: 50% seats are Home State Quota (HS) — reserved for students who passed Class 12 from that state. Home State cutoffs are significantly lower than All India cutoffs.

Example: MNIT Jaipur CSE — All India quota closing rank ~13,000. A Rajasthan student via Home State quota may get in at rank ~20,000–25,000. Always check HS quota cutoffs for your home state NIT.

CSAB Special Rounds — After JoSAA

After JoSAA Round 6, if seats remain vacant at NITs, IIITs and GFTIs, CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board) conducts 2-3 Special Rounds. These are for:

  • Students who could not participate in JoSAA
  • Students who want to try for remaining vacant seats
  • Generally easier to get seats — but options may be limited

Register at csab.nic.in for CSAB Special Rounds (July-August 2026).
