What to Do Based on Your NEET Score

🎉 Score 650+ / 720 — AIIMS & Top Govt Medical Colleges

Excellent score! You are in the top tier.

  • Score 680+ → AIIMS Delhi realistic (closing rank ~47-48 Gen)
  • Score 650-680 → Other AIIMS (Bhopal, Rishikesh, Jodhpur, Raipur)
  • Score 620-650 → Top state govt medical colleges (MAMC Delhi, KGMU Lucknow)
  • Register for MCC counselling at mcc.nic.in when it opens (July 2026)
AIIMS Cutoff Ranks → NEET Score Guide →

🔹 Score 500–650 — Good Govt Medical Colleges

Strong score — government MBBS is very much possible.

  • Score 580-650 → Mid-tier govt medical colleges via AIQ (All India Quota)
  • Score 500-580 → State quota govt colleges — home state advantage gives you lower cutoff
  • Register for both MCC (AIQ — 15% seats) and State counselling (85% seats)
  • Don't miss state counselling registration — more seats, lower cutoff than AIQ
Score vs College Guide →

⚠ Score 300–500 — Private Medical / BAMS / Alternative Paths

Government MBBS is difficult at this score but several strong options exist:

  • Private medical college govt quota seats — score 350-500 may qualify
  • BAMS (Ayurvedic Doctor): Score 300-400 gets govt BAMS colleges — legitimate, respected career
  • BHMS / BUMS: Similar cutoff to BAMS — Homeopathy and Unani medicine
  • BVSc (Veterinary) — NEET score used by most states, lower cutoff than MBBS
  • Consider drop year only if score was above 450 and improvement plan is realistic
BAMS / BVSc / Nursing Options → All Biology Career Paths →

❌ Score Below 144 (Gen) — Did Not Qualify

Below qualifying cutoff — MBBS/BDS/BAMS counselling not available this year. Options:

  • B.Sc Nursing: Does NOT require NEET. Direct admission based on Class 12 marks. Huge international demand — UK, Canada, Gulf.
  • B.Pharm: Does not require NEET. 4-year pharmacy degree. Good industry career.
  • BPT (Physiotherapy): NEET not required. Growing demand with India's sports boom.
  • Drop year: Seriously evaluate — 22 lakh students appear, only 1.18 lakh govt MBBS seats. Have a concrete study plan.
