Important — The NEET Reality Check

22 lakh students appeared for NEET 2025. Only ~1.18 lakh government MBBS seats exist across India. This means fewer than 1 in 18 NEET students gets a government MBBS seat. Private MBBS costs ₹60 Lakh – ₹2 Crore. Plan with this reality in mind — BAMS, Nursing, and B.Pharm are not fallback options, they are legitimate career choices with good futures.
