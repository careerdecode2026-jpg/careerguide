1. ICAR — Agriculture & Allied Sciences

ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research) is a Government of India body that oversees agricultural education across India. It offers admission to BSc Agriculture and allied programs through a centralised process at 74+ agricultural universities.

Important update (2026): ICAR UG admissions are now through CUET UG scores — not a separate ICAR exam. Students giving CUET can use their score for ICAR agriculture admissions. ICAR offers 15% seats at all State Agricultural Universities and 100% seats at ICAR-funded central institutes.

Top ICAR Institutes

IARI New Delhi
Indian Agricultural Research Institute — premier research institute. BSc + MSc + PhD in Agriculture. Nationally ranked #1 in agricultural research.
GBPUAT Pantnagar
G.B. Pant University of Agriculture — India's first agricultural university. Excellent placements in agri-business, FMCG, banking.
PAU Ludhiana
Punjab Agricultural University — strong in crop science, food technology. Good for students from North India.
TNAU Coimbatore
Tamil Nadu Agricultural University — excellent for South India students. Strong industry connections.

Career After BSc Agriculture

  • Government jobs: Agriculture Officer (state PSC), NABARD Officer, FCI, IARI Scientist, UPSC (Agriculture optional)
  • Banking: NABARD, SBI Agricultural Officer — dedicated agriculture banking roles
  • Agri-business: ITC Agri, Mahindra Agri, Bayer CropScience, IFFCO, Coromandel — sales and field roles
  • Food industry: Nestle, Britannia, ITC Foods — quality control, R&D
  • Higher education: MSc Agriculture → PhD → Faculty positions
  • Startup: AgriTech startups booming in India — Dehaat, Ninjacart, AgroStar
  • Starting salary: ₹25,000–40,000/month (Govt). ₹4–8 LPA (private sector). Agri-business sales: ₹6–12 LPA.

2. AYUSH — Full Clarification

AYUSH stands for Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy. It is a Ministry of the Government of India (Ministry of AYUSH) that oversees traditional medicine systems.

All AYUSH courses (BAMS, BHMS, BUMS, BSMS) require NEET UG from 2020 onwards. Counselling is done through AACCC (Ayush Admissions Central Counselling Committee) at aaccc.gov.in separately from MCC.

5. Biotechnology — BSc & BTech

Biotechnology combines biology with technology — genetics, molecular biology, pharmaceutical research, agriculture biotech, bioinformatics. Two routes available:

RouteProgramDurationAdmissionTop Colleges

Career After Biotechnology

  • Pharmaceutical R&D: Sun Pharma, Cipla, Biocon, Dr. Reddy's — drug development roles
  • Bioinformatics: Combining biology + data science — high demand in genomics companies
  • Agricultural Biotech: Developing disease-resistant crops, ICAR research positions
  • Medical Diagnostics: Developing test kits, PCR diagnostics (post-COVID, huge demand)
  • Higher Studies: MSc Biotechnology → PhD → Research positions in India or abroad
  • Starting salary: BSc Biotech ₹20,000–35,000/month. BTech Biotech ₹4–8 LPA. PhD/Postdoc: ₹50,000–1.2L/month (research stipend).

PCMB Strategy: Give both JEE Main AND NEET in the same year. Both exams are at different times (JEE Main Jan/Apr, NEET May) so there is no conflict. This gives you the maximum number of options — engineering + medical + agriculture + research all open simultaneously.

Unique Cross-Path Programs

BTech Bioinformatics
Combines Biology + Computer Science + Data Science. JEE Main or separate entrance. Emerging field with huge demand in genomics and pharma. Available at VIT, Amity, Manipal.
BTech Biomedical Engineering
Designs medical devices — MRI machines, pacemakers, prosthetics. JEE Main or BITSAT. Available at IITs (Kharagpur, Hyderabad), VIT, Manipal.
BTech Agricultural Engineering
Mechanization of farming — irrigation, food processing machinery. JEE Main + ICAR. Available at IIT Kharagpur, GBPUAT, state agricultural universities.
BSc in Data Science (Biological)
Computational biology, bioinformatics, genomics data analysis. Growing field — pharma companies and genomics startups actively hiring. IISER Kolkata, BITS Pilani.
