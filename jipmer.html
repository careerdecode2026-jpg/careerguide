About JIPMER

JIPMER (Jawaharlal Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education and Research) is a premier Central Government medical institute in Puducherry. It is an Institute of National Importance, similar in stature to AIIMS. JIPMER Karaikal is a second campus. Both admit students through NEET UG and MCC counselling.

No separate exam: JIPMER used to conduct its own entrance exam until 2018. From 2019 onwards, all JIPMER MBBS admissions are through NEET UG only.

Fee Structure

  • Annual tuition fee: approximately ₹6,500 per year
  • Hostel fee: approximately ₹10,000–15,000 per year
  • Total 5.5 year cost: approximately ₹1–1.5 Lakh (including hostel)
  • Extremely affordable — comparable to AIIMS

Why Choose JIPMER?

  • Institute of National Importance — globally recognized degree
  • Excellent tertiary care hospital — 2,000+ beds, diverse patient exposure
  • Strong research culture — publications from UG level
  • Good MD/MS options after MBBS within JIPMER itself
  • Puducherry is a pleasant city — good quality of life
