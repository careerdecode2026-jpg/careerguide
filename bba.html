DU JAT — Details

DU JAT (Joint Admission Test) is conducted by Delhi University for BMS (Bachelor of Management Studies), BBA Financial Investment Analysis, and BA (Hons) Business Economics at DU colleges.

DetailInformation
Pattern100 questions: Quantitative Ability (25) + Reasoning (25) + General English (25) + Business & General Awareness (25)
Marks+4 correct, -1 wrong. Total 400 marks. Duration: 2 hours.
Top CollegesSSCBS (Shaheed Sukhdev College of Business Studies), Deen Dayal Upadhyaya College, ARSD College
Cutoff (Gen)SSCBS typically requires top 200–500 rank. Score 300+ out of 400 for safe admission.
FeesDU government colleges: ₹20,000–40,000 per year. Very affordable.

Career After BBA

  • MBA from IIMs — BBA + CAT is the most common path to IIM. BBA graduates from good colleges get good CAT percentiles.
  • Direct corporate jobs — Sales, Marketing, HR, Finance roles at ₹4–8 LPA starting.
  • Banking exams — IBPS PO, SBI PO eligibility after any graduation.
  • Entrepreneurship — BBA provides business fundamentals for starting a venture.
  • CA after BBA — Can register for CA Intermediate directly (Direct Entry route) after BBA/BCom graduation.
