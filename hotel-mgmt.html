Hotel Management — NCHMCT JEE

Admission to 75+ Institute of Hotel Management (IHM) colleges run by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India. Strong international career potential in hospitality.

Eligibility

  • Class 12 passed from any recognized board — any stream
  • Minimum 50% aggregate in Class 12 (45% for SC/ST/PwD)
  • English must be one of the subjects in Class 12
  • Age: Maximum 22 years for General (25 for SC/ST/PwD)

Career After IHM

  • Hotel Industry — Front Office, F&B, Housekeeping, Kitchen Management at 5-star hotels (Taj, Marriott, Hyatt, ITC, Oberoi)
  • Starting salary — ₹3–5 LPA. Mid-career: ₹8–15 LPA. General Manager roles: ₹25–50 LPA
  • International opportunities — Hotel management has strong global mobility. IHM graduates work in Dubai, Singapore, Europe, cruise ships
  • Airlines & Hospitality — Cabin crew, airport lounges, airline catering
  • Food & Beverage industry — Restaurant management, catering companies
  • Higher education — MSc Hospitality Management abroad (Switzerland, Australia, UK)
