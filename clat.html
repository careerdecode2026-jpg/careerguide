What is CLAT?

CLAT (Common Law Admission Test) is the centralized entrance exam for 25 National Law Universities (NLUs) across India. It leads to a 5-year integrated BA LLB program. NLU Delhi conducts a separate exam called AILET — it is NOT part of CLAT.

All streams eligible: Science, Commerce, and Arts students can all apply for CLAT. No specific stream requirement — just Class 12 passed with minimum 45% (General/OBC) or 40% (SC/ST).

Career After LLB from NLU

  • Corporate Law Firms — AZB, Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas, Trilegal, Shardul Amarchand. Starting ₹12–20 LPA at top firms.
  • Litigation — High Court / Supreme Court practice. Income varies widely.
  • Judicial Services — State PSC exams for District Judge / Judicial Magistrate.
  • Civil Services (UPSC) — Law background is strong for IAS/IPS/IFS.
  • LLM Abroad — Harvard, Oxford, NYU for specialized law. Leads to international careers.
  • In-house Counsel — Companies like Google India, Amazon, ICICI Bank hire NLU graduates as legal advisors.
Rank tip: If CLAT rank is under 300 — target Tier 1 NLUs. Ranks 300–800 — target Tier 2. Beyond 800 — Tier 3 NLUs and private law colleges.
