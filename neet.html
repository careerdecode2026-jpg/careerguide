BDS — Bachelor of Dental Surgery

BDS is a 5-year dental degree (4 years + 1 year internship) admitted through the same NEET UG exam. Significantly lower cutoff than MBBS.

Counselling — How Seats Are Allotted

  • 15% All India Quota (AIQ) — Conducted by MCC (Medical Counselling Committee) at mcc.nic.in. All students compete nationally.
  • 85% State Quota — Conducted by respective state authorities. Only students with home state domicile eligible. Generally lower cutoff than AIQ for same college.
  • AIIMS seats — Fully under AIQ via MCC only. No state quota.
  • Number of rounds — Typically 3 MCC rounds + state rounds + stray vacancy round.
