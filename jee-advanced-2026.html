📄 How to Download JEE Advanced Admit Card

Admit card is available on the official JEE Advanced website. Follow these steps:

1
Go to jeeadv.ac.in — the official JEE Advanced website
2
Click on "Admit Card" link on the homepage
3
Login with your JEE Advanced registration number and date of birth
4
Download and print the admit card on A4 paper (colour or black & white both accepted)
5
Carry admit card + one valid photo ID (Aadhaar, School ID, or Passport) on exam day

✅ Exam Day Checklist — May 17, 2026

✓Printed admit card (both Paper 1 and Paper 2 on same admit card)
✓Original photo ID proof (Aadhaar / Passport / School ID)
✓Blue/black ballpoint pen (for rough work)
✓Transparent water bottle and light snack
✓Reach exam centre 30 minutes before reporting time
✓No mobile phones, smartwatches, or electronic devices inside
✓Paper 1: Morning session. Paper 2: Afternoon session. Both mandatory.
Important: Both Paper 1 and Paper 2 are mandatory on the same day. Skipping either paper will result in disqualification. Plan accordingly.

What to Do After JEE Advanced Result — June 1, 2026

Result will be declared on June 1, 2026 at jeeadv.ac.in. JoSAA counselling (for IIT + NIT seat allocation) starts June 2-3, 2026 — the very next day. Be ready.

🎉 Scenario 1 — You Got a Good IIT Rank

Congratulations! Here is what to do immediately:

  • Register on josaa.nic.in the moment it opens (June 2-3)
  • Fill college + branch preferences carefully — you can fill up to 300+ choices
  • Use "Float" option in early rounds to try for better IIT/branch while holding current allotment
  • Check IIT CSE closing ranks to know your realistic options
JoSAA Counselling Guide → IIT Cutoff Ranks →

⚠ Scenario 2 — You Qualified but Rank is Low (No Good IIT)

Don't worry — you still have excellent options:

  • Still register for JoSAA — newer IITs (Bhilai, Goa, Tirupati, Jammu) have closing ranks up to 16,000+
  • Even Civil/Chemical at older IITs is a respected degree — consider branch vs institute tradeoff
  • Your JEE Main rank also qualifies you for NITs — participate in JoSAA for both IIT and NIT preferences
  • BITS Pilani if BITSAT score was good — runs its own separate admission
JoSAA Process → NIT Cutoff Ranks → Rank vs College Guide →

❌ Scenario 3 — Did Not Qualify / Low Score

Your JEE Main rank still works for NITs, IIITs and GFTIs through JoSAA. This is not the end:

  • Register for JoSAA immediately — NIT seats are available based on JEE Main rank
  • BITSAT score separately opens BITS Pilani / Goa / Hyderabad
  • VITEEE / SRMJEEE / MET — good private colleges with decent placements
  • State CET counselling in your home state — affordable govt colleges
  • Drop year: Only if you scored above 90 percentile in JEE Main — realistic improvement possible
NIT Options → BITS Pilani → Private College Options → Decision Matrix →
