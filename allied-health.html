BAMS — Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery

BAMS is a 5.5-year degree (including 1-year internship) in Ayurvedic medicine. It is one of the most popular alternatives to MBBS for Biology students. BAMS doctors can practice, prescribe, and open their own clinics legally.

DetailInformation
AdmissionNEET UG (mandatory since 2020 for all AYUSH courses)
NEET Score NeededGovernment BAMS colleges: 300–450 / 720 (Gen). Private: 200–350 / 720
Total Seats~52,000+ BAMS seats across India (govt + private)
Govt College Fees₹10,000–50,000 per year. Total 5.5yr: ₹55,000–3 Lakh
Private College Fees₹1–5 Lakh per year. Total 5.5yr: ₹5–28 Lakh
CareerOpen your own Ayurvedic clinic, hospital practice, Panchakarma centers, wellness industry, AYUSH government posts
Salary (starting)Government AYUSH doctor: ₹35,000–60,000/month. Private practice: Variable — can be very high.

Similar options: BHMS (Homeopathy), BUMS (Unani), BSMS (Siddha) — all through NEET UG, similar cutoff and fee structure to BAMS.

B.Pharm — Bachelor of Pharmacy

DetailInformation
Duration4 years
EligibilityPCB or PCM in Class 12. Minimum 50% marks. NEET NOT required for B.Pharm.
AdmissionState-level pharmacy entrance exams (MHT-CET, KCET, UPSEE etc.) or direct merit-based admission at many colleges
FeesGovt college: ₹20,000–50,000/year. Private: ₹80,000–2 Lakh/year. Total 4yr: ₹1–8 Lakh
CareerPharmaceutical companies (drug manufacturing, QC, R&D), hospital pharmacy, retail pharmacy, drug regulatory affairs
SalaryStarting: ₹3–5 LPA. With M.Pharm / MBA (Pharma): ₹8–20 LPA. R&D roles at top pharma companies.
Top RecruitersSun Pharma, Cipla, Dr. Reddy's, Lupin, Biocon, Abbott, Pfizer, Johnson & Johnson

B.Sc Nursing — Government & Private

DetailInformation
Duration4 years (B.Sc Nursing) or 2 years (GNM — General Nursing & Midwifery)
EligibilityPCB in Class 12, minimum 45–50%. NEET not required for most nursing programs.
AdmissionAIIMS B.Sc Nursing has its own exam. State nursing entrance exams for govt college. Direct merit for private.
FeesGovt nursing colleges: ₹10,000–50,000/year. Private: ₹60,000–2 Lakh/year. Total 4yr: ₹40,000–8 Lakh.
CareerGovernment hospitals (AIIMS, ESIC), private hospitals, ICU/OT nursing, abroad opportunities (UK, Canada, Australia, Middle East)
International DemandIndian nurses are in extremely high demand globally. UK, Canada, Australia, Gulf countries actively recruit. Salary abroad: ₹4–10 Lakh/month equivalent.
International opportunity: Nursing is one of the few professions where India has a massive global demand. With B.Sc Nursing + NCLEX (for USA) or NMC (for UK), earning potential abroad is exceptional.

BVSc — Bachelor of Veterinary Science

DetailInformation
Duration5.5 years (5 years + 1 year internship)
EligibilityPCB in Class 12 mandatory. Minimum 50% marks.
AdmissionNEET UG score used by most states. Some states conduct separate vet entrance exams.
NEET Score NeededGovernment vet colleges: 400–520 / 720 (varies by state). State quota has lower cutoff.
Total Seats~6,000 BVSc seats across India
FeesGovt vet colleges: ₹15,000–80,000/year. Total: ₹1–5 Lakh. Private: ₹1–3 Lakh/year.
CareerGovernment vet officer (state/central), private clinics (pet care industry booming), poultry/dairy/livestock industry, FSSAI (food safety), research, defence (Army Veterinary Corps)
Govt salaryState vet officer: ₹40,000–80,000/month. Central govt: ₹60,000–1.5 Lakh/month.

BPT — Bachelor of Physiotherapy

DetailInformation
Duration4.5 years (4 years + 6-month internship)
EligibilityPCB in Class 12. NEET NOT required for most BPT programs.
Fees₹50,000–2 Lakh/year. Total: ₹2–10 Lakh.
CareerHospitals, sports physiotherapy (cricket/football teams), rehabilitation centers, private clinics. Growing demand with sports boom in India.
InternationalGood demand in UK, Canada, Australia for physiotherapists.
