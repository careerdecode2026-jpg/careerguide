What is CUET?

CUET (Common University Entrance Test) replaced board-based cutoffs for central university admissions from 2022 onwards. For Commerce students, CUET is the mandatory exam for admission to BCom (Hons), BA (Hons) Economics, and related programs at Delhi University (DU), JNU, BHU, Hyderabad University, and 250+ other central universities.

Key change: Class 12 board marks no longer determine DU admission. Only CUET score matters. A student with 85% in boards can get SRCC if CUET score is high enough.

Career After BCom / BA Economics via CUET

  • MBA from IIMs, ISB, XLRI (after graduation + CAT/GMAT)
  • Civil Services (UPSC) — Economics background is highly useful
  • Banking and Finance — IBPS, RBI Grade B, SBI PO
  • CA / CFA / CMA after graduation
  • Corporate roles in Finance, Consulting, Analytics
  • Masters in Economics abroad — LSE, Cambridge, US universities
