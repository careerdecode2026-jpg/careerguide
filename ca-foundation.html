UGC recognition: CA qualification is recognised by UGC as equivalent to a postgraduate (Master's) degree. A CA can apply for PhD programs and even faculty positions.

CA Foundation Eligibility

  • Register after passing Class 10 (provisional registration)
  • Appear for exam only after passing Class 12
  • All streams eligible — Science, Commerce, Arts
  • No minimum percentage in Class 12 required
  • No age limit

After CA Foundation — What Next?

  • Pass Foundation → Register for CA Intermediate
  • Complete CA Intermediate (both groups) → Register for 2-year Articleship under a practicing CA
  • During Articleship → Appear for CA Final
  • Pass CA Final + complete Articleship → Become a qualified CA (ICAI Member)

Average time to become a CA: 4.5 to 6 years after Class 12 (including Articleship). Pass rate at each level is ~20–35% — the exam is genuinely difficult.
