A Common Myth — Busted

"Arts students have fewer career options." This is completely false. Arts students have access to:

UPSC (the most prestigious exam in India) · Law (NLUs rival IITs in prestige) · Design (NID is world-ranked) · Psychology (fastest growing field) · Journalism · Fine Arts · Social Work · Teaching · Economics · Diplomacy · Theatre · Film

The diversity of career options in Arts is actually higher than in any other stream.
